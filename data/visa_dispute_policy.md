# Visa Transaction Dispute Policy (Internal)

## 1. Time Limits for Filing Disputes
- Cardholders must file a dispute within **60 days** of the transaction date. Disputes filed after 60 days are automatically denied.

## 2. Categorization of Disputes
- **Fraudulent Transaction:** The cardholder claims they did not authorize the charge. Requires blocking the card immediately. 
- **Merchant Non-Delivery:** The cardholder authorized the charge, but the goods/services were not received. Requires proof of contact with the merchant.
- **Subscription Cancellation:** The cardholder was charged for a recurring subscription after explicitly cancelling it.

## 3. Automated Approval Thresholds
- Disputes under **$50.00 USD** categorized as "Fraudulent Transaction" are automatically approved and credited, provided the account is older than 6 months and has no prior fraud claims in the last 30 days.
- Disputes over **$50.00 USD** or matching "Merchant Non-Delivery" require manual review or full transaction history validation.
